Agentify Architecture Map

Every component of the platform, and each data flow traced one at a time. Pick a flow on the left: the components it touches light up, its hops are numbered on the map, and the ordered steps appear below with the real endpoint each one calls.

Why one flow at a time, and not all of them at once. There are 16 flows over 18 components. Drawn simultaneously that is roughly 90 edges — a hairball that answers nothing. So the map stays static and legible, and the flows are overlaid on demand. This is a structural view: it shows what exists and which parts each flow touches, not precise ordering or concurrency. For strict sequence and timing, see docs/SEQUENCE_FLOWS.md, which holds eight sequence diagrams.
